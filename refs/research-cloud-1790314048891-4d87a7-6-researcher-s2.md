abstract only.

The source material from Kumar et al., 2024 [S1] explicitly reports a per-interval retention rate metric over 100 update intervals under controlled conditions with bounded token budgets and known perturbations. This establishes a measurable, experimentally grounded basis for evaluating retention dynamics in tokenized agents. However, the provided excerpts do not detail how this metric is computed or how perturbation effects are isolated across intervals, leaving contextual interpretation incomplete. Oracle Agent Memory [S2] introduces a memory mechanism with structured lifecycle phases but does not report or define a per-interval retention rate metric, thus not contributing to the targeted evaluation framework. The evidence confirms the existence and scope of the requested metric in one source, while others introduce different memory architectures without alignment to the specified metric or conditions.
