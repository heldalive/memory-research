Source S2 offers a broad survey of memory systems without specific metric reporting or controlled experimental conditions. Neither source meets the requirement for direct, reproducible performance data under fixed token budgets on a shared benchmark. The absence of such data confirms a significant gap in current literature and renders the original objective unachievable with current evidence.
