# SWEnergy: An Empirical Study on Energy Efficiency in Agentic Issue Resolution Frameworks with SLMs

[Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.09543v2)

abstract only.

Source [S1] reports token usage and energy consumption in agentic frameworks using SLMs but focuses on energy inefficiency and reasoning loops, not memory decay or token retention trends. Source [S2] observes persistent errors in memoryless agents over days, indicating state drift, but provides no quantified data on token reduction per update interval or its relationship to performance degradation. The requested data from Kumar et al., 2024, Section 4.1 is absent, preventing any evaluation of token retention trends under periodic input as required by the research objective. Thus, the claim of a measurable correlation between token reduction and state drift lacks explicit support in the available evidence.
