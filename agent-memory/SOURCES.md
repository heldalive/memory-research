# Annotated source catalog

Accessed 2026-09-16. This is a broad, living review, not a claim to include every paper. Abstract-level entries are explicitly marked; experiments and full-method reproduction remain separate. Provider and author performance claims are not independent verification.

## S01 — Memory in the Age of AI Agents

- Kind: survey. Read level: abstract. Status: retrieved.
- Source: [Memory in the Age of AI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13564)
- Research relevance: Organizes memory by form, function and dynamics; a map of the field, not a winning algorithm.

## S02 — Generative Agents

- Kind: paper. Read level: abstract. Status: retrieved.
- Source: [Generative Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442)
- Research relevance: Observation, retrieval, reflection and planning in a simulated society; believability is not factual-memory accuracy.

## S03 — MemGPT

- Kind: paper. Read level: abstract. Status: retrieved.
- Source: [MemGPT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08560)
- Research relevance: An agent manages context and external memory tiers; extra tool calls introduce cost and failure modes.

## S04 — Reflexion

- Kind: paper. Read level: abstract. Status: retrieved.
- Source: [Reflexion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11366)
- Research relevance: Stores verbal feedback from attempts; test procedural improvement separately from factual recall.

## S05 — Voyager

- Kind: paper. Read level: abstract. Status: retrieved.
- Source: [Voyager](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16291)
- Research relevance: Reusable executable skills in Minecraft; skill transfer is domain-specific and distinct from remembering facts.

## S06 — MemoryBank

- Kind: paper. Read level: abstract. Status: retrieved.
- Source: [MemoryBank](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10250)
- Research relevance: Long-term conversational memory with updating and forgetting; early personalization architecture.

## S07 — A-MEM

- Kind: paper. Read level: abstract. Status: retrieved.
- Source: [A-MEM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12110)
- Research relevance: Dynamically linked notes inspired by Zettelkasten; evaluate whether links help beyond an equal-budget retrieval baseline.

## S08 — Mem0

- Kind: paper. Read level: abstract. Status: retrieved.
- Source: [Mem0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.19413)
- Research relevance: Extraction, consolidation and retrieval, with a graph variant. Author-reported benchmark gains require matched-model reproduction.

## S09 — Zep

- Kind: paper. Read level: abstract. Status: retrieved.
- Source: [Zep](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13956)
- Research relevance: Temporal knowledge graphs for agent memory; validity time and provenance matter for changing facts.

## S10 — HippoRAG

- Kind: paper. Read level: abstract. Status: retrieved.
- Source: [HippoRAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14831)
- Research relevance: Graph-based retrieval inspired by associative memory; retrieval is one part of an agent-memory system.

## S11 — HippoRAG 2

- Kind: paper. Read level: abstract. Status: retrieved.
- Source: [HippoRAG 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14802)
- Research relevance: Studies deeper integration of passage retrieval and graph reasoning; do not equate QA retrieval with lifelong learning.

## S12 — Hindsight

- Kind: paper. Read level: abstract. Status: retrieved.
- Source: [Hindsight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12818)
- Research relevance: Retain, recall and reflect architecture with typed memories. Published scores are claims from its evaluation setup.

## S13 — LightMem

- Kind: paper. Read level: abstract. Status: retrieved.
- Source: [LightMem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18866)
- Research relevance: Lightweight memory with staged processing and offline consolidation; account for background maintenance cost.

## S14 — ACE: Agentic Context Engineering
