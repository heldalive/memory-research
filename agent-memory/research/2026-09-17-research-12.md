## Question
What evidence, if any, shows that a practical agent-memory method improves retention, retrieval, updating, or contradiction handling over a simple Markdown, transcript, or full-history baseline when context/token budgets are comparable?

## Scope
Inspect at most four primary sources (papers, benchmark papers, official technical reports, or maintained project documentation authored by the system creators). Include approaches beyond skills and Markdown, but admit a source only when it describes an operational memory method and reports evidence, evaluation design, or a concrete comparison. Practitioner commentary may identify leads but is not an eligible evidence source. Do not search indefinitely.

Outcome values: `eligible source found`, `lead only`, `duplicate`, `inaccessible`, or `no eligible result`. Record the selected source and query number that discovered it. Stop immediately after four eligible sources, or after all six queries have been run and screened if fewer than four qualify.

## Eligibility and inspection procedure
For each candidate, record title, authors/organization, date (if available), canonical HTTPS URL, source type, access date, and inspection depth (`discovered`, `abstract screened`, or `source inspected`). A source counts only after reading the relevant primary text; do not count search snippets, citations, or unverified repositories.

Inspect and record exact section, page, table, figure, or heading identifiers for:

- storage representation and scope;
- write, update, consolidation, and provenance policy;
- indexing and retrieval procedure;
- forgetting, deletion, staleness, and contradiction handling;
- comparison baseline (especially Markdown/files, transcript retrieval, or full-history context);
- context/token, latency, model, and dataset budget conditions;
- author-reported metrics, results, ablations, and failure cases;
- stated limitations and threats to validity.

Use this per-source record:

`Source ID | discovery query | canonical URL | exact inspected locations | storage | write/update | retrieval | forgetting/contradiction | baseline | budget comparability (yes/no/unclear + evidence) | author claim (quotation/paraphrase) | our inference | limitations | inspection depth`.

Never describe results as reproduced. Mark any missing field `not reported`; do not infer it from implementation assumptions. Preserve negative or null findings.

## Comparison and acceptance criteria
The loop is accepted when the record contains:

1. A complete query log for all queries actually run, with an outcome for each.
2. No more than four eligible primary sources, each with a canonical HTTPS link and precise inspection locations.
3. Every required storage, policy, retrieval, forgetting/contradiction, baseline, budget, evidence, claim/inference, and limitation field completed or explicitly marked unreported.
4. A per-source judgment of whether the baseline comparison is budget-comparable (`yes`, `no`, or `unclear`) supported by cited text.
5. A synthesis that distinguishes author-reported evidence from our inference and states whether superiority is supported, unsupported, or unresolved. Simple baselines may win; no ranking is assumed.
6. An auditable stopping statement naming either “four eligible sources reached” or “all six predefined queries screened,” plus the unresolved gaps.
