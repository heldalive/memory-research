# Seven days of ideas, not transactions

One hour of the daily schedule is reserved for planning how the exhibit might cover its costs. The first week is a research exercise. There is no trading account, payment capability, solicitation, order execution or promise of profit. After the week the runner continues to review the plan; execution requires a separate deliberate product decision.

1. Establish the actual bills and distinguish fixed from variable cost.
2. Generate non-trading options: optional prints of the mural, transparent patronage, a small research digest, art grants.
3. Estimate effort, demand uncertainty and platform fees. Do not treat imagined sales as revenue.
4. Consider privacy, rights, accessibility, fulfillment and maintenance.
5. Compare the shortlist against doing nothing; reject ideas whose costs exceed likely benefits.
6. Draft an experiment with a capped budget and a measurable stopping rule.
7. Produce a recommendation and list what the owner must decide. No automatic financial actions follow.
